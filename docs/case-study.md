# From coordination problem to operational CRM

## Discovery
The original problem was organising volunteers for outside broadcasts: availability was gathered through email chains. Working within a radio station revealed a wider issue: membership records, competitions, newsletters and event coordination were spread across manual processes and disconnected tools.

## Delivery
RadiYO! centralised contacts and roles, membership renewals, email communication, competition entries and event staffing. The product was developed using PHP, JavaScript, MySQL and Bootstrap, with Claude and ChatGPT supporting development. The first station adopted the product after demonstrations and three training sessions.

## Results
Founder-reported observations from the first station include ten membership renewals in one day following a targeted renewal email and approximately 100 contacts added through competitions. A typical weekly competition received approximately 50 entries. These are operational observations, not controlled causal measurements or AI-specific outcomes.

## Lessons
Discovery and adoption matter as much as implementation. The competition workflow replaced a paper-based draw with eligibility-aware random selection; membership segmentation made targeted communications practical. The product's voice synthesis integration provides a further example of applying AI to a broadcast workflow. Its deployment status and impact have not been independently measured in this showcase.
